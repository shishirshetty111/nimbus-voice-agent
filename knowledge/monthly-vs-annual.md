# Monthly vs annual billing

We offer both monthly and annual billing cycles, with annual plans receiving a 20 percent discount. We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and wire transfers for enterprise accounts exceeding $5,000 USD. All transactions are processed in USD, though local currency display options are available in the billing dashboard.

## How it works

- **Monthly billing:** pay the monthly price each month; cancel any time and the account stays active until the end of the current cycle.
- **Annual billing:** pay for 12 months up front at a discounted per-month price.
- Both monthly and annual plans are covered by the 30-day money-back guarantee.
- Free tiers cost nothing either way; Enterprise tiers are custom-priced.

**Annual billing saves 20% on every paid tier except Nimbus Docs Starter (25%), Nimbus Boards Starter (25%), where the annual price is rounded. Always quote the per-tier figure from the table.**
